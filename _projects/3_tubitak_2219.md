**Development of Model-Based Continual-Reinforcement Learning Mechanisms for Aerial Robots to Enable Rapid Adaptation to
Unexpected Changes and Disturbance Effects**

_Hava Robotları İçin Beklenmedik Değişikliklere ve Bozucu Etkilere Hızlı Adapte Olmasını Sağlayan Model-Tabanlı
Sürekli-Pekiştirmeli Öğrenme Mekanizmalarının Geliştirilmesi_

**Funding:** TÜBİTAK 2219 International Postdoctoral Research Fellowship Program<br>
**Researcher:** Dr. Ali Tahir Karasahin<br>
**Host:** Dr. Basaran Bahadir Kocer, Aerial Robotics Group, Bristol Robotics Laboratory, University of Bristol<br>
**Duration:** July 2024 – July 2025

Aerial robots are expected to keep performing well as the conditions of their environment and their payload change.
Transporting a suspended payload is a demanding example: the tether dynamics depend on wind, payload mass and flight speed,
and modelling them requires expert knowledge and detailed mathematical models.

This research developed learning mechanisms that let an aerial robot adapt quickly to sudden changes in a suspended payload
and in its operating environment. In the training phase, the dynamic model parameters and adaptation parameters are updated
across a range of tasks. In the test phase, the learned parameters are used to evaluate performance on both previously seen and
unseen tasks.
