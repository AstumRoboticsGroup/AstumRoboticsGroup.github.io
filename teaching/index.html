Graduate Courses

Reinforcement Learning for UAVs PhD

The principles and applications of reinforcement learning (RL) for unmanned aerial vehicles: decision-making under uncertainty, policy and value function approximation, and learning-based control. Students design, train and evaluate RL-based UAV control systems in simulation, with attention to sensor feedback, autonomous mission planning and real-world deployment.

Lecture notes

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles MSc

The components, design principles and applications of UAVs, and how to use UAV systems to solve engineering problems through control systems, sensor integration, mission planning and simulation tools.
