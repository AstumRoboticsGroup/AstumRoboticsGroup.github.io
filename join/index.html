Join Us

PhD, MSc, undergraduate and visiting opportunities in the ASTUM Robotics Group at Necmettin Erbakan University.

We welcome enquiries from students and researchers who would like to join the group. We design, model and control aerial robots and other mechatronic systems. Our work covers the design of aerial robots and the test stands used to characterise them, controller performance and disturbance rejection, reinforcement learning for aerial robot control, aerial grasping and perching. We build hardware and evaluate our methods in real-world experiments.

A background in mechatronics, mechanical, electrical or aerospace engineering, computer engineering or a related field is helpful, as is experience with any of the following: control and estimation, embedded systems, PX4 or ROS, simulation, or machine learning.

PhD and MSc Students

We take on a small number of graduate students each year through the Necmettin Erbakan University graduate programmes. Funded positions on research projects will be posted here when they are available. Candidates with their own scholarship, for example from TÜBİTAK (2210 or 2211 programmes) or from their government or employer, are also welcome.

Please send

  • a CV and your degree transcripts;
  • a short statement of research interests: which of our themes interests you, and what you would like to work on;
  • the programme and funding route you have in mind;
  • a thesis, report or paper you have written, if available.

Email subject: "Graduate enquiry – Your name".

Undergraduate Students

Final-year design projects, TÜBİTAK 2209 projects and research internships are available each year. If you would like to work on aerial robots, get in touch early in the term.

Visiting Researchers

We welcome visiting students and researchers when there is a clear joint project. Please contact us a few months ahead.
